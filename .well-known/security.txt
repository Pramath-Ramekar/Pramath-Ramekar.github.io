# Security notes

This is a static portfolio site. There is no login, no session, no database, and no
server-side code. Nothing submitted here is stored.

Reported vulnerabilities in the site itself are welcome. Reports are handled directly
by me; there is no bounty programme for this site.

For vulnerabilities in AI systems, products or research, see the case studies on the
blog page. Responsible disclosure reports for those go through HackerOne or Bugcrowd
rather than to this address.
